Research question

How can we tell when generated knowledge is dependable?

I am interested in evaluation frameworks that go beyond surface fluency to examine whether model outputs are grounded in evidence, contextually coherent, and robust across different generation workflows.

My work combines empirical analysis, benchmark construction, LLM-based and human evaluation, and system-level experimentation.
